Sales & onboarding process

How a Smart Cloud deal moves from first conversation to a connected, live customer. Shared by Melita and Smart Technologies. Target times are proposals for the teams to agree.

  1. 1

    Pre-Sales

    Owner: Sales (Melita or Smart Technologies)

    A lead is registered in the portal as soon as there is a real opportunity.

    • Record customer, contact, service type (VPS, Backup, VPS + Backup, Bespoke), originating partner and owner.
    • Record monthly recurring value, one-off value, term and probability %. The dashboard calculates total and weighted contract value.
    • Keep next step, notes and comments current; update probability after every customer interaction.

    Exit: customer confirms (signed order / PO). Sales completes the technical onboarding sheet and submits it.

  2. 2

    Technical Review

    Owner: Technical team · Target: 2 working days

    Submitting the sheet marks the deal as won (100%), locks the sheet and notifies the technical team.

    • Technical team checks the sheet against the Smart Cloud / Melita Business catalogue and capacity.
    • Approve → onboarding starts and its checklist is created automatically.
    • Request changes → sheet goes back to sales with comments; sales updates and resubmits (new version).
